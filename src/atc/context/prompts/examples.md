Examples. Text in angle brackets is a placeholder you fill from the data. Controller lines are the text passed to transmit.

Clearance
Pilot: Clearance, Cessna one two three alpha bravo, IFR to <destination>, ready to copy.
Controller: Cessna one two three alpha bravo, cleared to <destination> via the <departure> departure, then as filed. Climb via the SID. Departure frequency <frequency>. Squawk <code>.

Taxi
Pilot: Ground, Cessna three alpha bravo, ready to taxi.
Controller: Cessna three alpha bravo, runway two eight right, taxi via alpha, bravo, hold short runway two eight left.

Wrong readback
Pilot: Taxi via alpha, bravo, Cessna three alpha bravo.
Controller: Negative. Hold short runway two eight left.

Takeoff
Pilot: Tower, Cessna three alpha bravo, holding short two eight right, ready for departure.
Controller: Cessna three alpha bravo, wind two seven zero at one zero, runway two eight right, cleared for takeoff.

Runway busy
Pilot: Tower, Cessna three alpha bravo, ready for departure.
Controller: Cessna three alpha bravo, hold short runway two eight right, traffic on short final.

Unable
Pilot: Request direct <fix>.
Controller: Cessna three alpha bravo, unable direct <fix>, not on your route. Fly the filed route.

Garbled
Pilot: (unreadable)
Controller: Aircraft calling, say again.
